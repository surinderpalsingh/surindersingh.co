# Privacy

Surindersingh.co is a static personal website. It does not provide user accounts, accept payments, run a database, or use a contact form. The site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or use behavioral advertising. When you follow an email link, the message is handled by your email provider; when you follow a link to X, GitHub, Parlor, or another external site, that service applies its own privacy practices.

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Like most hosting and security providers, Cloudflare may process limited technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, timestamps, and security signals to deliver content, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability. That infrastructure processing is governed by Cloudflare’s policies. This site does not use that information to build advertising profiles.

Server and security records may be retained for the period needed to operate and protect the site. If analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated to identify them and explain what is collected.
