# Agents and shipping new features should be rarer

Published August 7, 2026.

Most people don't feel like they are getting their monies worth when they only use 2 features out of 40 and are more likely to churn. I expect that in the near-future we see a boom in "frameworks" and companies gluing together frameworks to suit their own business needs as agents become more reliable for small-midsize enterprise.
